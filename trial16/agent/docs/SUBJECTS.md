# Subjects

Subjects belong to one collection; collections are owned by different
archives and never share subjects.

Two links name the same subject when, within a collection and category,
their titles are equal after trimming, collapsing runs of whitespace to one
space and ignoring case. "Washington" the person and "Washington" the place
are different subjects (different categories).

A subject keeps the spelling it was first created with.

Titles are in many languages (German, Turkish, Greek archives).
