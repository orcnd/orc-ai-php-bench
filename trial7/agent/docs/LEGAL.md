# Legal requirements (DE/FR customers)

* Invoice numbers are sequential without gaps. A validated invoice may only
  be deleted while it is the latest number and unpaid (the number is then
  reused); otherwise it is cancelled with a credit note. Allowing arbitrary
  deletion creates gaps and fails tax audits.
* Bookkeeping (GoBD): posted journal entries are never modified or deleted.
  Corrections are booked as a reversal of the original entry plus a new
  entry, so the audit trail shows the original amount.
