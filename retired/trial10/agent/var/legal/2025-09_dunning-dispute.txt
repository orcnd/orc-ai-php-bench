Letter from Kanzlei Weber & Partner, 12 Sep 2025 (excerpt)

Our client transferred EUR 100.00 without designating a debt. Under
§ 366 (2) BGB such a payment discharges the debt that fell due first. Your
system spread the amount over all three invoices, left all of them partly
open and charged dunning fees on each. The dunning fees are void; please
book the payment against invoice INV-0981 (due 1 Aug) as the law requires.
