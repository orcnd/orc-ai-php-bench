# #87 (closed: works as intended) Short time entries cost less than expected

"7 minutes at 90/h should be 10.50 but the invoice says 10.80."

Resolution: by design, see ADR 0004. 7 min = 0.12 h, 0.12 x 90.00 = 10.80.
The portals reject lines where quantity x price differs from the amount.
We tried computing from minutes in release 3.2 and had to roll it back
after two customers' portals rejected every invoice with short entries.
